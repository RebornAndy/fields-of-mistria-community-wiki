## Profile {#profile}

Adeline is Mistria's town reeve. She balances official duties with a sincere wish to see the town thrive again, and she appreciates neighbors who follow through on their promises.

## Gifts {#gifts}

Thoughtful gifts and carefully made items are a good way to show Adeline that the farm is becoming part of the community. A small, well-chosen present is more meaningful than an extravagant gesture with no context.

## Schedule {#schedule}

Most days, Adeline can be found around Mistria Manor or attending to town business. Her routine changes for festivals and town events, so check the calendar before planning a visit.
